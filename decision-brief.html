Africa's Talking · Manufacturing Hackathon · Nairobi · 27 Aug 2026 Decision: Build
Team decision brief

Build the Order-to-Cash Agentproblem #8

The winning shape for this event is not a machinery problem. It is an event-triggered agent that talks to the people who live outside our software (customers, suppliers, debtors) over SMS, USSD, Voice and M-Pesa. Order-to-cash is the sharpest, safest, most reusable version of that.

01

The decision

BUILD — Order-to-Cash agent

An agent for SME and Jua Kali manufacturers that takes an order, confirms and schedules it, collects payment by M-Pesa, and chases late payment with an escalating SMS then a Voice call. Ship it Marketplace-ready. Procurement / restock (#2) is the same engine as a second module.

Why it wins this specific hackathon

Gate 1Demoable with no fake IoT. A real phone rings in the room. Nothing to simulate.
Gate 2Marketplace-horizontal. Every manufacturer has receivables. One env var (M-Pesa shortcode) and a stranger clicks Deploy.
Gate 3Named in the brief. Order Management and Manufacturing Finance are 2 of the 12 official categories.

05

What we are not building

Kill the "production disruption" agent

  • No real trigger. Without IoT, a human types "machine down" and the agent is just a notification router.
  • Fails the Marketplace test. It needs each factory's machines, schedules and customers. No stranger can click Deploy.
  • Cannot demo honestly. The event has to be faked, and judges see through fake IoT.

The only valuable, buildable piece inside it is the communication loop. We keep that piece and give it a real trigger: an order and an invoice due date. That is #8.

06

Before we commit — ask the floor in hour one

The one load-bearing assumption

That owners will trust an agent to contact their own customers automatically, and that those customers respond to automated SMS, USSD and Voice instead of ignoring it. If a "no", pivot to the supplier side (#2), where the trust concern is lower.

Three questions that can change the plan

  • Walk me through the last time a customer paid late. What did you do, and how many hours did it cost?
  • Would you let software SMS or call your customers on your behalf, or is that too risky?
  • In today's sandbox, what is most reliable to demo live: inbound SMS, USSD, or Voice callbacks?
