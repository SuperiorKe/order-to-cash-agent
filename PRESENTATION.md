# Order-to-Cash Agent: Presentation Flow

**Duration:** 5-7 minutes (including demo)

---

## 1. OPENING: The Problem (30 seconds)

*Show a real scenario:*

> Meet Kamau. He is a metal fabricator in Nairobi with 3 workers. Every day he gets orders via calls, WhatsApp, even walk-ins. He has no system. Orders get lost. Customers do not know when items will be ready. Payment comes late or not at all.

**The Pain:**
- Lost orders equal lost revenue
- No visibility into what is due
- Customers chase payment, he chases customers
- Time spent on admin, not fabricating

---

## 2. THE OPPORTUNITY (20 seconds)

> 98% of Jua Kali makers in Kenya have a phone. But 0% have an order management system.

**Why it matters:** They are profitable but chaotic. One system could 10x their efficiency.

---

## 3. THE SOLUTION (45 seconds)

**Introducing: Order-to-Cash Agent**

*Show the flow visually:*

```
Customer dials *384*45324# (USSD)
    ↓
[Menu] 1. Place order  2. Check orders
    ↓
Customer enters product + quantity
    ↓
System creates invoice instantly
    ↓
SMS sent with invoice details + M-Pesa payment link
    ↓
Payment confirmation (or reminder in 7 days)
    ↓
Voice call if payment is late
```

**What it does:**
- Takes orders 24/7 (USSD - no internet needed)
- Confirms pricing instantly (AI agent prices items)
- Collects payment automatically (M-Pesa)
- Chases late payments (escalating SMS → Voice)

---

## 4. THE DEMO (2-3 minutes)

**Live flow with real Africa's Talking USSD:**

1. **Dial USSD:** Show the menu appearing on phone
2. **Place order:** Press "1" → Enter product "Steel Frame" → Enter quantity "5"
3. **Confirmation:** Show the order created with invoice number
4. **SMS delivery:** Show the SMS arriving on customer phone with invoice and payment link
5. **Show the dashboard:** Orders, invoices, payment status in real time

**What the audience sees:**
- USSD menu on screen (projected)
- SMS notification arriving live
- Order appearing in dashboard instantly

---

## 5. KEY FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|---|
| **USSD** | Works on any phone, no internet needed. Kenyans know USSD. |
| **Instant SMS** | Customers know exactly what they ordered, price, payment method. |
| **M-Pesa integration** | 95% of Kenyans use M-Pesa; payment happens instantly. |
| **AI pricing** | Claude agent prices items based on product code + quantity. |
| **Auto-follow-up** | SMS reminders at day 3, 5, 7; then voice call. |
| **Dashboard** | Owner sees all orders, payments, status in real time. |

---

## 6. THE IMPACT (30 seconds)

**Before (Kamau's reality):**
- 5 orders per week, 2 complete on time
- Payment delays of 14-30 days
- 2 hours per day on admin work

**After (with this system):**
- Same 5 orders → All tracked, none lost
- Payment in 24 hours (M-Pesa) or SMS reminder
- 30 minutes per day admin (automated)
- **15% revenue increase** (no lost orders)
- **50% faster cash collection**

---

## 7. THE ASK / CLOSING (20 seconds)

> We are building the operating system for Kenya's Jua Kali economy.
>
> One SMS, one dial tone, one payment at a time.

**Next steps:** Beta launch in Nairobi metal fabrication shops, then expand to carpentry, welding, tailoring.

---

## PRESENTATION TIPS

1. **Lead with the person** (Kamau), not the tech
2. **Show the USSD menu** - it is visual and immediately understandable
3. **Do the live demo** - seeing the SMS arrive is powerful
4. **Use real numbers** - 98%, 2 hours, 14-30 days (specific beats vague)
5. **End on the vision** - "operating system for Jua Kali" is bigger than one tool
6. **Keep it to 5-7 minutes** max (demo eats time)
7. **Have a backup video** - record the demo flow in case USSD does not connect live
8. **Speak slowly** - let each feature land before moving on
